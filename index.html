Structural Disruption: Robustness Stress-Testing for Vision Models
Oct — Dec 2025

Problem

Vision models can look accurate on standard benchmarks while still being fragile to structural, compositional changes in an image.

Approach

Used SAM segmentation and DINOv2 attention maps to locate high/low-attention regions, then applied two attack strategies — blacking out high-attention regions and swapping semantically meaningful parts — to generate out-of-distribution samples.

Evaluation

Designed a novel Structural Divergence Score (GradCAM + SSIM) and benchmarked ResNet18 and EfficientNetB0 on accuracy, confidence, and decision-boundary stability.

Result: confidence dropped from 46% → 28% under perturbation, with SDS scores of 0.2109 confirming strong out-of-distribution behavior.
